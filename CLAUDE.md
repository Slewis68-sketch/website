# Working rules for Claude in this repo

## Plan first
Sessions here start in plan mode (`.claude/settings.json`). Propose the plan and wait for approval before doing anything.

## Paid credits and outside services
Before spending any paid credits (Krea, Google Flow / Veo, Bloom, any API), state:
1. the tool and model,
2. **which account is billed** (Krea vs. Google vs. other),
3. the cost and the balance left,

then wait for an explicit yes. Never assume which account pays.

Before publishing or sharing anything outside this session (pushing personal photos, sharing links, posting), ask first.

## Video work
See `ai-video-fx/`: `FLOW_BATCH.md` (clip prompts), `batch.sh` (render all effects), `script.json` (captions).
